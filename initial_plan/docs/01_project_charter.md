# Project Charter: DPDP_Secure
## 1. Problem Statement (LS-04)
Indian MSMEs lack accessible, affordable, and lightweight tools to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023). Existing enterprise solutions require heavy cloud subscriptions and complex IT infrastructure.

## 2. Mission & Core Objective
Deliver an offline-first, zero-setup privacy compliance platform tailored for Indian small and medium businesses.
